The Poetry of the Grain

01 / OUR PHILOSOPHY

Craftsmanship as Meditation

At Timber Trace, we believe that wood is a living ledger. Every ring, knot, and swirl tells the story of a century spent reaching for the light. Our role is not to impose a shape, but to listen to what the timber wishes to become.

In an age of instant gratification and mass production, we choose the difficult path. We choose the hand-plane over the industrial sander. We choose the chisel over the CNC machine. This intentional slow-down allows the artisan to connect with the material at a cellular level, ensuring each piece carries the warmth of the human touch.

"A hand-carved edge is never perfectly straight, and that is where its soul resides."

— ELIAS VANCE, FOUNDER
Sourcing Ethics

Honoring the Forest

Sustainability isn't a buzzword for us; it is our foundation. We exclusively source fallen timber from managed Scottish estates or reclaimed wood from historic architecture. By salvaging wood that would otherwise be forgotten, we give new life to centuries of growth.

02 / THE ARTISAN

Meet the Maker

Elias Vance began his journey in a drafty shed behind his grandfather’s house in the Highlands. Trained in traditional joinery, he spent a decade perfecting the Japanese technique of *Shou Sugi Ban* and the delicate art of dovetail joinery.

"Every piece I make is a dialogue," Elias says. "The wood resists, I negotiate. Eventually, we reach an understanding. That understanding is the finished piece of furniture."

Patience.

We age our raw timber for an additional six months in the workshop air before a single cut is made.
