Michael is the sole proprietor of Timber Trace Crafts (timbertracecrafts.com). He makes and sells laser cut/engraved wooden jewelry, boxes, and crafts. He sells on Etsy and plans to expand to eBay and Amazon Handmade. He is not a developer by trade — he wants a complete, maintainable store that he can run alone.

**Preferences:**
- Server-rendered pages, minimal JS (Alpine.js only where needed)
- Fastest possible page loads — LiteSpeed caching is a priority
- Laravel is the agreed framework
- Hostinger shared hosting business plan (LiteSpeed, PHP up to 8.5, MySQL)
- Hostinger's built-in SMTP for transactional email
- Stripe for payment processing (switched from Square)
- Sender.net for marketing email (may switch if needed)
- Umami.js for analytics
- Pirate Ship for shipping labels (Phase 2)

**How to apply:** Keep solutions simple and maintainable for a solo operator. Avoid over-engineering. Prefer built-in Laravel features over third-party packages where possible.
