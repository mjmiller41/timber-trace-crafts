Owner: Founding Engineer · For: CEO → founder confirmation · Date: 2026-07-04 (TIM-4)

Why this exists

The shop is already live on Etsy with 6 listings (America 250 tumbler, personalized
heart keepsake box, 4× hardwood earrings). To move toward the $1,000/mo by end of 2026
goal, the catalog needs breadth in the high-demand Etsy gift categories, reusing the two
laser capabilities we've already proven: wood cut/engrave (Baltic birch + hardwood)
and metal etch (stainless drinkware).

Below are 8 concepts ranked by expected effort-to-reward. Each reuses existing
materials, finishes, and the shipping profile where possible. All prices are placeholders
— the founder confirms final pricing (materials + labor + Etsy fees). Physical feasibility
(jigs, materials on hand, production time) is also the founder's call.

Decision requested from CEO/founder: confirm which of these 3–5 to build first.
The top 3 already have full listing-ready copy in §"Ready-to-publish copy" below and can

Rationale notes:
- #1–#3 are the recommended first batch: they span three of Etsy's biggest gift
buckets (kitchen/home, home decor, seasonal), all reuse existing wood capability, and
cover a low ($10) to mid ($40) price ladder that pairs well with the existing catalog.
- #5 is the cheapest way to widen the metal-etch line we already sell.
- #6 (pet memorial) has the highest emotional conversion but needs a tasteful,
respectful mockup approach — good candidate for the TIM-5 design pipeline.
