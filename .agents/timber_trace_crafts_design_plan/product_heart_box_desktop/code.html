The Timber Trace Philosophy

Rooted in Tradition, Refined by Light.

Every piece that leaves our workshop in the Pacific Northwest represents over forty hours of meticulous design and hand-finishing. We believe that the objects we surround ourselves with should possess a soul—a quiet resonance born from honest materials.

The 'Forever Infinity' series was inspired by the delicate architecture of dried eucalyptus leaves and the geometric perfection found in natural honeycombs. It is designed to age gracefully, acquiring a unique patina as it passes through generations.
